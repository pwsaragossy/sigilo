Enrolment — the key that stays yours

Spending inside the pool means proving membership in the allow-list, and that proof is built from a secret only this wallet's key can derive. The issuer never holds it. So enrolment is something a holder consents to — and it is checkable: the leaf the policy gate inserted should be the one this wallet derives.

Prove one payment

The problem every confidential balance creates: if nothing is readable, nothing can be shown to an accountant, a counterparty or a regulator. Pick one payment. What leaves this wallet proves that payment and discloses nothing else — not the balance, not the other notes, not who else was paid.

Verify it — no wallet required

This panel holds no keys, opens no storage and has no account. It takes a receipt and an RPC URL, and answers. That is what makes a disclosure worth giving: the recipient needs nothing from you afterwards.

One wallet page, one exclusive local database — close the issuer / investor / auditor demo before opening this, or the second tab evicts the first. Proving is real work: ~9–13 s of Groth16 per operation, in a worker, not simulated.
